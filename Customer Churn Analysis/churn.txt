What is “Customer Churn”?

Customer churn means:

Customers leaving a company, canceling a subscription, stopping purchases, or becoming inactive.

Example:

Netflix user cancels subscription
Bank customer closes account
Telecom customer switches network
SaaS user stops renewing plan
E-commerce customer never buys again

That is called churn.





Simple Formula

Churn Rate=
Customers Lost/Total Customers×100

Example

Suppose:

Company has 1000 customers
150 customers leave

Then:

Churn Rate=
150/1000×100=15%




TYPES OF CHURN
1. Voluntary Churn

Customer leaves intentionally.

Example:

expensive pricing
bad experience
better competitor
2. Involuntary Churn

Customer leaves unintentionally.

Example:

payment failure
expired card
technical issue
